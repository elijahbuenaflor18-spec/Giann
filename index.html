Honest Ones

The questions worth asking

Before you ask them, here are our honest answers.

"What if Vyzee doesn't hit ₱1M MRR?"
Then you've still closed retainers and earned commission on every one. Equity is the upside; your monthly take is never zero. The downside case is a well-compensated sales role.
"Why 2%, and not more?"
The cap table is being built now, Shad and Jaja are co-founders, Cha leads growth at the same 2%. You're entering at parity with the Head of Growth, not below her.
"Why is this equity story different?"
A productized SKU with proven unit economics, a vertical with 2,500+ targets, and a guarantee that forces delivery. Operator equity, not random stuff.
"What's my base before ₱100k MRR?"
Commission-led with a modest stabilizer until founder draws activate at ₱100k MRR — projected Month 3. Earned commission alone matches your previous role by Month 4.
"What if the founding team breaks down?"
Your vesting and equity are protected by the founder employee agreement, independent of who stays. The cap table protects you from us, not just us but from you.
"Why me, why now?"
We explained the role to you on April 30. And I already chose you. The cliff starts on signature day. The seat exists. It just doesn't have your name on it yet.
